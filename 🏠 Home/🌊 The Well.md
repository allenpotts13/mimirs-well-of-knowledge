## 📜 The Philosophy of the Well

> [!tip]
> ### One Note = One Idea
> Keep knowledge focused, reusable, and easy to connect.

> [!note]
> ### Link Knowledge
> Understanding grows through connections. Do not duplicate information when it can be linked.

> [!important]
> ### Templates First
> Consistency makes knowledge easier to navigate, maintain, and expand.

> [!warning]
> ### Experience Matters
> Documentation explains *what*. Experience explains *why*. Preserve decisions, mistakes, lessons, and reasoning.

> [!success]
> ### Future You Comes First
> Knowledge preserved today should make tomorrow easier.
