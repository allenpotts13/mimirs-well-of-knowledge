# <span class="rune">ᚱ</span> Network+: CompTIA Network+ (N10-009)

> "A+ teaches you the machine. Network+ teaches you what happens the moment it talks to another one."

---

## 🎯 What is Network+?

The natural next certification after CompTIA A+, validating the knowledge and skills to establish, maintain, and troubleshoot wired and wireless networks — from OSI fundamentals through routing, switching, cloud connectivity, security, and structured troubleshooting.

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Level | Early-career networking |
| Format | Multiple choice/multiple answer, performance-based |
| Length | 90 minutes, max 90 questions |
| Passing score | 720 / 900 (commonly cited — verify current figure) |
| Recommended experience | CompTIA A+, plus 9-12 months in networking |

> [!warning] Confirm before booking
> CompTIA updates exam format, pricing, and skills outlines periodically — double-check current details on the [official Network+ exam page](https://www.comptia.org/certifications/network) before scheduling.

## 📚 Concepts

The five domains measured on the exam:

- [[OSI Model, Appliances, and Cloud Concepts]] — OSI layers, appliances/functions, cloud vocabulary *(part of 23%)*
- [[Ports, Protocols, Media, and Topologies]] — ports/protocols, transmission media, topologies *(part of 23%)*
- [[IPv4 Addressing and Modern Network Architectures]] — subnetting, SDN, zero trust, IPv6 transition *(part of 23%)*
- [[Routing and Switching Configuration]] — routing technologies, VLANs, STP *(part of 20%)*
- [[Wireless Configuration and Physical Installation]] — wireless devices, rack/power/environment *(part of 20%)*
- [[Network Documentation, Change Management, and Disaster Recovery]] — documentation, EOL/EOS, RPO/RTO, DR sites *(part of 19%)*
- [[Network Monitoring and Access Management]] — SNMP, SIEM, VPN, in-band/out-of-band *(part of 19%)*
- [[DNS, DHCP, and IP Network Services]] — DHCP, DNS records, time protocols *(part of 19%)*
- [[Network Security Fundamentals and Compliance]] — AAA, encryption, CIA triad, compliance *(part of 14%)*
- [[Network Attacks and Defense Techniques]] — Layer 2 attacks, NAC, ACLs *(part of 14%)*
- [[Network Troubleshooting Methodology and Overview]] — the 7-step methodology *(24% — the largest domain)*
