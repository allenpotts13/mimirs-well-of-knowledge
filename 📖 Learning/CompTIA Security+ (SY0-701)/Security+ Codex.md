# <span class="rune">ᛉ</span> Security+: CompTIA Security+ (SY0-701)

> "Network+ taught you how systems talk to each other. Security+ teaches you every way that conversation can be turned against you — and how to stop it."

---

## 🎯 What is Security+?

The industry-standard entry point into cybersecurity, validating baseline knowledge across security concepts, threat/vulnerability management, architecture, day-to-day security operations, and program-level governance and risk management.

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Level | Early-career security |
| Format | Multiple choice/multiple answer, performance-based |
| Length | 90 minutes, max 90 questions |
| Passing score | 750 / 900 (commonly cited — verify current figure) |
| Recommended experience | ~2 years in IT administration with a security focus |

> [!warning] Confirm before booking
> CompTIA updates exam format, pricing, and skills outlines periodically — double-check current details on the [official Security+ exam page](https://www.comptia.org/certifications/security) before scheduling.

## 📚 Concepts

The five domains measured on the exam:

- [[Security Controls and Fundamental Concepts]] — control types, CIA, AAA, Zero Trust *(part of 12%)*
- [[Change Management and Cryptographic Solutions]] — change management, PKI, encryption, hashing *(part of 12%)*
- [[Threat Actors and Attack Vectors]] — actor types, motivations, threat vectors *(part of 22%)*
- [[Identifying Vulnerability Types]] — application, OS, hardware, cloud, mobile vulnerabilities *(part of 22%)*
- [[Attack Indicators and Mitigation Techniques]] — malware, network/app/password attacks, mitigations *(part of 22%)*
- [[Architecture Models and Infrastructure Security]] — architecture models, network appliances *(part of 18%)*
- [[Data Protection, Resilience, and Recovery]] — data states, HA, DR sites, backups *(part of 18%)*
- [[Hardening and Asset Management]] — secure baselines, mobile deployment, asset lifecycle *(part of 28%)*
- [[Vulnerability Management and Security Monitoring]] — CVE/CVSS, SIEM, monitoring tools *(part of 28%)*
- [[Enterprise Security Capabilities and Identity Access Management]] — email security, IAM, MFA *(part of 28%)*
- [[Automation, Incident Response, and Digital Forensics]] — automation, IR process, forensics *(part of 28%)*
- [[Security Governance and Risk Management]] — governance hierarchy, risk formulas *(part of 20%)*
- [[Third-Party Risk and Compliance]] — vendor agreements, compliance elements *(part of 20%)*
- [[Audits, Assessments, and Security Awareness]] — audit types, pen testing, awareness *(part of 20%)*
